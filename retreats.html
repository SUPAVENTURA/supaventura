The Experience

More Than a Retreat

This is not just a yoga retreat. It is a complete experience where adventure, movement and wellness come together.

SupAventura is built on small groups. Maximum 8 people per trip — so every experience feels personal, connected and intentional. This is not a mass retreat. It's a curated week designed around you.

Throughout the week you'll explore Santa Teresa, practice yoga, learn to surf, discover hidden waterfalls, visit Tortuga Island, enjoy nourishing meals and share unforgettable moments with an amazing community.

Come alone or with friends. Many guests travel solo and arrive without knowing anyone — and leave with new friendships.

No surfing experience. No yoga experience. Just bring your curiosity. 18+ only.

Questions

FAQ

Do I need yoga or surf experience? +
No experience needed at all — not in yoga, surfing or anything else. Our instructors adapt to every level, from complete beginners to those with some experience. Just bring an open mind.
Can I travel alone? +
Absolutely — most of our guests travel solo! You'll arrive as a stranger and leave with new friends. Community is at the heart of the SupAventura experience.
What's included in the price? +
Everything is included: 7 nights accommodation at Zeneidas Beach House, all meals (7 breakfasts, 6 lunches, 7 dinners + welcome dinner), 4 surf lessons with video analysis, Isla Tortuga boat trip, bioluminescence experience, ATV waterfall adventure, yoga sessions, SUP yoga, sound healing, cacao ceremony, new moon ritual, welcome gift, and all local transportation to activities.
What airport should I fly into? +
Fly into San José (SJO — Juan Santamaría International Airport). From there you have two options: a small plane (avioneta) to Cobano + taxi to the house, or a shuttle + ferry that drops you right at the door of Zeneidas. We'll help you coordinate your arrival.
How do I get to Santa Teresa? +
From San José you can take a small plane (avioneta) to Cobano (~30 min) and then a short taxi to Zeneidas, or take a shuttle + ferry from San José that arrives right at the house. Both options are comfortable — we'll send you full details once you reserve.
What are the accommodations like? +
You'll stay at Zeneidas Beach House, a beautiful beachfront property surrounded by jungle. The house has 3 rooms with bunk beds (one room for 2 with A/C, one for 4 with A/C, and one for 2 with fan and ocean view), a shared bathroom, full kitchen, and common areas. Note: the house may also have other Zeneidas Garden guests outside the retreat group.
How do I reserve my spot? +
Message us on WhatsApp to reserve your spot. A $500 deposit confirms your place. Payment plans are available — ask us and we'll find a solution that works for you. Balance is due before arrival.
